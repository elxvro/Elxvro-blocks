# ELXVRO Blocks v0.12.0 — Material Fracture Rebuild

Target: make line/column clears feel like real material breakage while preserving the original menus, modes, profile, store, missions, settings and progression.

## Incremental rebuild
- v0.11.1 — Glass: exposed-edge fracture, inward crack propagation, shard separation and refraction flash.
- v0.11.2 — Wood / Stone: fibrous splinter edges, stone cracks, chunks and dust.
- v0.11.3 — Leaf / Crystal / Marble: torn fibers, drifting leaf fragments, crystal facet shards, marble chips and gold-vein fracture.
- v0.11.4 — Multi-stage timing: crack -> break -> debris -> fade, plus material-specific launch physics and particle trails.
- v0.11.5 — Fracture propagation: clear contour travels through the actual cleared cells; heavy materials add more board impact.
- v0.12.0 — Finalized material fracture engine, longer readable timing, performance-safe particle caps and signed Android test build.

The visual rebuild is intentionally isolated from navigation and game-state logic.
