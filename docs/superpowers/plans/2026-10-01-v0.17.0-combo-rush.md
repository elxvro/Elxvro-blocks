# ELXVRO Blocks v0.17.0 Combo Rush Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add Combo Rush as a separate 120-second mode with bounded combo multipliers and independent best score/best combo persistence.

**Architecture:** Keep the existing board engine and `GameScreen` flow. Put Combo Rush scoring rules in a small pure helper so timing/combo math is testable; extend `GameMode`, `AppState`, mode selection, HUD status, and local record displays without altering existing Timed records.

**Tech Stack:** Flutter 3.47.4, Dart, `SharedPreferences`, `flutter_test`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-v0.17.0-impact-update-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Combo Rush is additive; existing `GameMode.timed` and its record remain unchanged.
- Combo Rush duration is exactly 120 seconds.
- A scoring clear increments the combo chain; a legal placement with no clear resets it to zero.
- Multiplier tiers are exactly: combo 0–1 = 1.00, combo 2 = 1.15, combo 3 = 1.30, combo 4 = 1.50, combo 5+ = 1.75 cap.
- Combo Rush best score and best combo persist independently.
- Existing save keys must not be renamed.

## Review Focus

- A placement that clears two lines is still one combo step, not two; pinned in Task 1.
- A non-clearing legal placement resets combo even if the prior chain was high; pinned in Task 1.
- Combo 5, 6, and 20 must all cap at x1.75; pinned in Task 1.
- Loading old installs with no Combo Rush keys must default both records to zero; pinned in Task 2.
- Existing 2-minute Timed best score must not be read or overwritten by Combo Rush; pinned in Tasks 2 and 3.
